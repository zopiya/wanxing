朱砂印章 · The cinnabar seal

A brand mark that behaves like a chop.

Red base. White stamp punched out of it. 4-pixel radius — never sharper, never softer. The contrast is absolute and does not adapt to dark mode.

It is not a UI icon. It is a signature that the page wears once.
